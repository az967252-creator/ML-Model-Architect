# Group 04 — Neural Networks

## 1. Overview
Deep networks: MLP, CNN, RNN/LSTM, Transformer.

## 2. Family Members
MLP, CNN, RNN/LSTM, Transformer

## 3. Strengths
- Captures complex patterns
- SOTA on image/text
- Transfer learning

## 4. Weaknesses
- Data-hungry
- Compute-intensive
- Black-box

## 5. Recommended Baseline
Small MLP for tabular; pretrained CNN for vision

## 6. Key Hyperparameters
learning_rate (1e-5 to 1e-2), batch_size (32-512), epochs (10-200), dropout (0.0-0.5) (heuristic, no source)
