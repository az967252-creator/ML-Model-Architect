# Group 07 — k-NN

## 1. Overview
k-Nearest Neighbors: instance-based, lazy learning.

## 2. Family Members
KNeighborsClassifier, KNeighborsRegressor

## 3. Strengths
- No training phase
- Simple and intuitive

## 4. Weaknesses
- Expensive at inference
- Curse of dimensionality

## 5. Recommended Baseline
KNeighborsClassifier (k=5)

## 6. Key Hyperparameters
`n_neighbors (3-51), weights (uniform/distance)`
