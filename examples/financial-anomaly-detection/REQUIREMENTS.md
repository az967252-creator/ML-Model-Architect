# Requirements — Financial Anomaly Detection

## Goals
### Goal 1: Detect Anomalous Transactions
- What: Flag transactions that deviate from normal spending behavior.
- Decision supported: System blocks the transaction and alerts the fraud team.
- Success metric: Reduce fraud losses by 20% in 6 months.
- Error cost: False Negative is worse (we lose money). False Positives are acceptable but must be kept under 2%.
