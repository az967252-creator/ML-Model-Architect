# Requirements — Car Price Prediction

## Goals
### Goal 1: Predict Used Car Prices
- What: Predict the price of a used car based on age, mileage, engine size, and brand.
- Decision supported: Dealership uses the model to set fair listing prices.
- Success metric: RMSE < $3,000.
- Error cost: False Positive (overpricing) leads to no sale. False Negative (underpricing) loses money.

## Requirements
- Data: Historical car sales data.
- Features: Age, mileage, engine_size, brand.
